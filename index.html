Who We Are

Kids Who Show Up

The Sutton Dudes are a crew of kids who love bringing people together. We plan events, build stands, and lend a hand wherever the community needs one — on bikes, on scooters, or on foot. If there's a way to help, we're finding it.

What started as a few friends wanting to do something good has turned into a full-on crew with a simple rule: show up, pitch in, and have fun doing it.
